Privacy First Guarantee

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

LabelLens was founded on the strict principle that healthcare and nutritional intelligence must never come at the expense of personal privacy. As an educational science fair project, our architecture is engineered from the ground up to ensure that your data never leaves your device.

1. Local & Edge Computation

All barcode parsing, optical image inspection, Nutri-Score 2.0 calculations, and ingredient analysis are executed directly on your client device (in your local web browser or on local exhibition kiosk edge hardware). We do not transmit captured food packaging photos or video frames to any remote cloud servers.

2. No Tracking & No Third-Party Telemetry

  • No Analytics: We do not include Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No Account Required: You never need to sign up, provide an email address, or create a password to use LabelLens.
  • No Cookies: We do not store persistent tracking cookies on your browser.

3. Camera Access

When you grant permission to use your smartphone or webcam camera, the video stream is processed exclusively in temporary volatile memory using client-side JavaScript (ZXing barcode library) or your local offline Ollama vision server. Video streams are never recorded or saved to disk.

4. Open Food Facts Live Fallback

If a scanned barcode is not found in our offline 115+ benchmark catalog, the web app queries the public Open Food Facts API (a non-profit, open nutritional registry) directly from your browser. Only the numeric barcode number is queried.

5. Educational & Scientific License

LabelLens is an open-source educational initiative. For questions or security audits, contact us at science.exhibition.labellens@example.com.
