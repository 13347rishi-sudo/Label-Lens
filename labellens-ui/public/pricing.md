# Pricing & Open-Access Terms — LabelLens

## Software License & Cost Structure

LabelLens is a 100% Free and Open-Source Public Health Science Exhibition Project. There are no paywalls, recurring subscriptions, API keys, or premium tiers.

### General Public & Educational Tier
- **Price**: $0.00 / month (Free forever)
- **Included Features**:
  - Full European Nutri-Score 2.0 algorithmic calculation engine
  - Complete 115+ benchmark offline product database
  - Real-time Open Food Facts global barcode fallback
  - 50+ hidden sugar alias analysis
  - Industrial palm oil derivative tracker
  - 14 EU major allergens scanner
  - European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) E-number chemical inspector
  - 5 Personalized health profile lenses (Diabetic, Allergy, Child, Hypertension, Adult)
  - Interactive live reformulation sliders
  - Side-by-side food comparison duel arena
  - Mobile phone camera barcode and QR code scanner
- **Usage Limits**: Unlimited scans, unlimited comparisons.
- **Privacy Policy**: 100% local computation. No user tracking, no image uploads to cloud servers, no account required.
